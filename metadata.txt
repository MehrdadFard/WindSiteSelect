about=Wind Site Select is a GIS-based wind energy site selection tool for professional GIS users. It supports AOI definition, wind and depth/bathymetry screening, spatial filtering with exclusion and inclusion constraints, and multi-criteria analysis (MCA) with criteria weights, raster/vector outputs, and optional Excel/CSV reporting. It does not perform bankable energy-yield calculations and does not replace engineering, environmental, grid-connection, or permitting studies. Users must supply appropriate source datasets; no regional sample rasters are selected automatically.
